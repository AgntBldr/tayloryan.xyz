Taylor Ryan Portfolio Product Review

Decision-focused site review, technical discovery upgrade, and ICE-ranked roadmap. Updated 14 July 2026.

Executive Summary

The site has broad proof, but it asks visitors to interpret too much of it. The strongest next conversion lever is finishing and restructuring proof, especially case studies, before adding more portfolio categories.

The technical discovery pass is now strong: canonical metadata, social previews, crawler controls, favicons, structured data, and a current sitemap are generated from one manifest. The remaining product work is primarily positioning, proof quality, and information architecture.

Completed In This Pass

  • Open Graph and Twitter metadata for LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Slack, and other link unfurlers.
  • Unique page titles and descriptions drafted by local/lower-tier models and checked deterministically.
  • Canonical URLs, robots directives, homepage Person/WebSite structured data, and current XML sitemap.
  • Branded SVG, PNG, Apple touch, and ICO favicon assets.
  • Four raw overview fragments removed from indexing; duplicate email routes redirected.
  • Twenty-two high-use images compressed from 15.09 MB to 2.98 MB while preserving dimensions.
  • Homepage links normalized and key icon-only controls given accessible names.

Current Product Assessment

  • Discoverability: 8/10. The technical foundation is now complete; search performance still depends on content quality and external authority.
  • Offer clarity: 6/10. The homepage establishes experience but mixes leadership, advisory, speaking, and builder offers.
  • Proof quality: 5/10. The volume is strong, but placeholder case-study content and generic testimonials weaken trust.
  • Conversion path: 7/10. Contact and booking are visible; visitors still need clearer fit and next-step expectations.
  • Usability/performance: 6/10. Image payload is improved, while runtime CDN dependencies and archive-heavy navigation remain.
