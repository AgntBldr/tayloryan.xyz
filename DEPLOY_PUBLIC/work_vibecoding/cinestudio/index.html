Case study

From problem to working product

CineStudio organizes characters, locations, objects, and creative direction into an eight-second video workflow using image and video models. It makes the dependencies of a complex generation visible before the expensive step runs.

The problem

Generative video can consume time and credits quickly when references, scene direction, and model requirements are assembled through trial and error.

The response

The product turns the prompt into a structured production brief, prepares the supporting image context, and then runs the video model through a secure key flow.

Workflow

How the system moves

01

Build decisions

Choices that shaped the product

  • Structure the brief before invoking the more expensive video model.
  • Keep API keys out of the client bundle and repository.
  • Explain model and billing requirements at the point where they affect the workflow.

What it demonstrates

Proof beyond the interface

  • End-to-end product design for emerging image and video models.
  • Secure handling of model access in a prototype environment.
  • Ability to organize complex creative dependencies into a clear workflow.
